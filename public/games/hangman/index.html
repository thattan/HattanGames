    You need at least two players: one to set the phrase, one to guess.

    How to play

    1. Each round one player is picked to type a phrase. Everyone else guesses it together.
    2. Every turn lasts 30 seconds. Click a letter to vote for it, or propose a full-phrase guess and others can back that instead.
    3. Whatever has the most votes when the clock runs out is submitted. Ties are settled by coin flip, and the turn ends early once everyone has voted.
    4. A wrong letter or a wrong phrase guess costs a limb. Six and the group is hanged.

    It's co-op: the phrase setter scores nothing, so there's no reason to pick something nobody can get. The results screen shows how many rounds the room solved and who backed the right calls.
