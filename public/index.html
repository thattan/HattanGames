Gym Class

Pick a game. Grab your friends. No download, no account.

  • Dodgeball

    Throw, catch, and bank it off the wall.

    Up to 8 players, two-minute rounds. Hit someone to score; catch their throw to score and knock them out.

    2-8 playersPlay

  • Hangman

    Vote a letter. Don’t hang the group.

    One player sets a phrase, everyone else gets 30 seconds a turn to vote on the next letter — or gamble on solving it outright.

    2-40 playersPlay

  • Sharks & Minnows

    Get to the far wall. Don’t get caught.

    Twenty seconds to cross the gym while the sharks patrol the middle. Get tagged and you turn shark too — last minnow standing wins.

    2-16 playersPlay

  • Tic Tac Toe

    Three in a row, best of the series.

    Two players, 20 seconds a move. X moves first and the X seat swaps every round, so neither of you keeps the advantage.

    2 playersPlay
