    Everyone starts as a minnow except a few sharks. On the whistle you have 20 seconds to reach the painted wall on the far side. Make it and you score a point and sit the rest of the crossing out safely.

    Get tagged and you turn shark on the spot — so the last few seconds of a crossing are the dangerous ones. Sharks score a point per tag and can never set foot in either safe wall, though the wall you started behind only shelters you for the first three seconds.

    Still in the water when the whistle goes? The sharks got you. Play continues, direction reversed, until one minnow is left against the pack.

    Both sides get a dash. The shark’s goes further; the minnow’s recovers faster, so a well-read juke beats a lunge.
