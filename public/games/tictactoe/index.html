    How to play

    Three in a row wins the round — across, down, or corner to corner. X always moves first, and the X seat swaps every round so neither of you keeps the first-move advantage.

    You get 20 seconds a move. Run the clock out and a random empty square gets played for you, so nobody can stall the match. The results screen counts it against you.

    Most rounds won takes the match. Draws count for neither of you, and an even series is an even series — there's no tiebreak.
